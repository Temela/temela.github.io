About Me

I am a Catholic priest and data scientist with deep roots in theology and information technology. I focus on responsible AI, data ethics, and human-centered computing—especially within underserved and aging populations.

Research

My work explores AI fairness, predictive health models, digital communication systems for the Church, and inclusive technologies. I've contributed to projects on biometric-integrated voting, cardiovascular disease prediction, and real-time edge analytics for IoT.

Research Interests: Healthcare Analytics, Human-Centered Computing, Data Ethics, Machine Learning, AI Fairness, Distributed Systems

Bio
