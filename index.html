About Me

I am a Catholic priest and data scientist with deep roots in theology and information technology. I focus on responsible AI, data ethics, and human-centered computing—especially within underserved and aging populations.

Research

My work explores AI fairness, predictive health models, digital communication systems for the Church, and inclusive technologies. I've contributed to projects on biometric-integrated voting, cardiovascular disease prediction, and real-time edge analytics for IoT.

Research Interests: Healthcare Analytics, Human-Centered Computing, Data Ethics, Machine Learning, AI Fairness, Distributed Systems

Bio

Born and raised in Nigeria, I was formed intellectually and spiritually through my studies in philosophy, theology, and technology. As a Spiritan priest, I serve both in the pastoral and digital mission fields, driven by the vision of a just and compassionate society empowered through data-driven insights. I direct the Spiritan Healthcare Apostolate, where I work to improve the lives of aging clergy and vulnerable individuals through predictive analytics and tech-supported care systems. My research has earned recognition for its practical impact and ethical depth, including an award from the Spiritan Generalate in Rome. I continue to bridge the worlds of code, conscience, and communion.
